FAQ

01

What are the benefits of an Aquafacial treatment?

Aquafacial treatments provide numerous skin benefits, including:

- Improved skin volume – Achieve smooth skin, a lean jawline, full jowls, and high cheekbones.
- Boosted cell turnover – Supports exfoliation, hydration, and skin renewal with retinoids and a healthy diet.
- Enhanced skin texture & tone – Gives your skin a more vibrant and youthful glow.
- Sun damage repair – Helps reduce the effects of sun exposure on the skin.
- Acne reduction – Effectively reduces or eliminates acne for clearer skin.
- Rejuvenated eye area – Improves the skin around the eyes for a refreshed appearance.

02

What types of laser hair removal do you offer?

We offer Diode, Nd:YAG, and Alexandrite laser hair removal, all of which are FDA & Health Canada approved.

- Diode Laser (800-810 nm) – Suitable for light to medium skin tones but can also treat darker skin with the right settings.
- Nd:YAG Laser (1064 nm) – Best for darker skin tones, as it penetrates deeper while protecting the skin’s surface.
- Alexandrite Laser (755 nm) – Ideal for lighter skin tones, offering fast and effective hair removal.

💡 Each laser is tailored to different skin and hair types—our specialists will recommend the best option for you!

03

What is Microdermabrasion, and how does it work?

Microdermabrasion is a non-invasive skincare treatment that exfoliates and removes dead skin cells, revealing smoother and brighter skin. It involves a tiny diamond-tipped device that resurfaces the skin, improving texture, reducing fine lines, and stimulating collagen production. It’s highly effective for treating acne scars, sun damage, and uneven skin tone.

What are the benefits of microneedling serums?

We offer a variety of Toskani microneedling serums tailored to different skin needs:

- Regenerating Cocktail – Improves skin renewal, fades acne marks, has anti-inflammatory and soothing properties, and prevents dehydration.
- Anti-Aging Cocktail – Prolongs Botox effects, reduces wrinkles, provides a lifting effect, and stimulates cellular metabolism.
- Radiance Cocktail – Corrects hyperpigmentation, lightens brown spots, and has an intensive detoxifying and antioxidant action.
- A-OX 360 Cocktail – Contains powerful antioxidants to prevent acne and premature aging, while increasing hydration and skin elasticity.
- Polivitamin BCAE – A vitamin-rich solution (A, C, E, and B group) that nourishes, revitalizes, and protects the skin from UV rays and oxidative stress while promoting cell renewal.

What is the Skinderma Exo-Ageless treatment?

Skinderma Exo-Ageless is designed for mature, damaged, and dry skin with deep wrinkles. This treatment deeply nourishes the skin, helping restore elasticity, reduce wrinkles, and enhance hydration for a more youthful and revitalized appearance.

06

What’s the difference between AquaFacial and HydraFacial?

The difference is minimal—both treatments deeply cleanse, exfoliate, and hydrate the skin, leaving it refreshed and glowing. HydraFacial is a trademarked version with patented technology, while AquaFacial follows a similar process but may vary slightly depending on the provider. The effects and benefits are essentially the same, so you can expect radiant, hydrated skin with either treatment!
